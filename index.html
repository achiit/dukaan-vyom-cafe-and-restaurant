Meet Vyom

I started this cafe 4 to 5 years ago in a small shop on Gali Number 9 because there was nowhere nearby for a proper, honest breakfast. My family still comes by on Sundays for the aloo paratha.

Many of our regulars are patients' families and staff from Kailash Hospital. They need a hot meal and a warm cup of chai without any fuss, and that is what we try to give them.

  • Everything is cooked fresh when you order
  • Honest prices, no hidden charges
  • Right opposite Kailash Hospital
  • Owner is on the counter every day
